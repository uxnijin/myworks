How It Operates

Simple, mathematical color blending rules.

A

Tint Blending

Gradually blends chosen base channels with white (RGB 255).

B

Shade Blending

Gradually blends chosen base channels with black (RGB 0).

C

Linear Steps

Interpolates intervals evenly to prevent color jumps.
