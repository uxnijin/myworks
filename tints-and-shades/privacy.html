Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

This privacy policy describes how we collect, protect, and use user information in the Tints & Shades Figma plugin. We are committed to protecting your privacy. The plugin runs entirely inside Figma's sandboxed iframe environment, and we do not collect, process, or send any of your file colors or design assets to external servers.

Data Collection & Processing

Tints & Shades does not require user registration or profile tracking. It runs purely on the frontend of your local computer. When you select colors and click export options, the code calculates the RGB color variations entirely in-memory.

No analytics cookies, tracking scripts, or database queries are utilized, ensuring that your corporate design libraries remain fully private.

Figma Integration Compliance

The plugin's operations and variables binding functions fully comply with Figma API terms of service and user data policies. We request access strictly to edit selection nodes when you choose to "Save Styles" inside your active document canvas.

Security & Safety

All temporary user configurations (such as your chosen steps count) are saved within Figma's client storage sandbox (`figma.clientStorage`). Since no data leaves the Figma client, your design files are completely secure from external breaches.

Contact

If you have questions or feedback regarding this policy, please reach out to us at privacy@uxnijin.com.
