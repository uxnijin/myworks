Common questions

Does Still really shoot RAW on a non-Pro iPhone?

Yes. Apple ProRAW is a Pro-model feature, but the underlying Bayer RAW capture is available on nearly every iPhone. Still finds it and writes a standard DNG negative. Open Settings › Capabilities in the app to see exactly what each lens on your phone supports.

Why does the zoom change when I turn RAW on?

A DNG is always the whole sensor readout, so digital zoom cannot be baked into one. With RAW armed, the zoom row drops to the native lens stops your phone actually has, and pinch is ignored — otherwise the negative would not match the frame you composed.

Where are my DNG files?

In your photo library, alongside the processed image. Still also keeps its own copy in the Files app, under On My iPhone › Still › RAW.

Do my edits change the original negative?

Never. Still writes edits to a small separate file beside the DNG and re-renders from the original every time. Exports are always new files, and clearing an edit restores the original exactly. The negative is never opened for writing.

Some modes are missing on my iPhone.

Portrait only appears on phones with real depth hardware. Rather than shipping a cut-out that looks like a cut-out, Still hides the mode where the hardware cannot do it properly.

Can I shoot from the Lock Screen?

Yes, on iOS 18 and later. Add the Still control to Control Centre, the Action Button, or either Lock Screen button slot. Pressing it on a locked phone opens Still's capture screen without unlocking.
