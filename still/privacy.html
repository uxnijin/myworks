Privacy Policy

Still · Last updated 13 August 2026

Still does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data. It has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. The app contains no networking code at all, so your photos cannot leave your iPhone unless you share them yourself.

What Still accesses, and why

Camera. Still needs camera access to show a viewfinder and take photographs. Frames are processed entirely on your device.

Photo library. With your permission, Still saves the photographs and DNG negatives you take to your photo library, and reads it to show your recent shots in the corner thumbnail and the in-app grid. Still only ever reads your library in order to display it back to you.

On-device storage. Still keeps a copy of each DNG negative, together with a small text file recording any edits you have made, inside its own folder on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What Still does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or user identifiers
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No network requests — the app ships no networking code
  • No data sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because none is collected

Children

Still collects no data from anyone, including children.
