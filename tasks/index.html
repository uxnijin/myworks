Chrome Extension

A beautiful way to manage your Google Tasks

Tasks is a lightweight browser extension that lets you view, create, and organize your tasks directly from your browser toolbar. Fully synced with Google Tasks.

Add to Chrome — It's Free

Real-Time Google Sync

All edits, updates, and additions sync instantly to your Google Account. Access your tasks across Gmail, Google Calendar, or Google Tasks on mobile.

Minimalist Design

A beautiful distraction-free popup interface designed with elegant typography and clean layouts to help you focus on your actual work.

Privacy by Default

We respect your data privacy. The extension uses direct Google OAuth API communication. We do not run any servers, and we never collect or view your tasks.

Keyboard Friendly

Speed through your lists. Press enter to create a task, space to complete it, and escape to clear. Works smoothly without taking your hands off the keyboard.
