Designs

App and product design: onboarding flows, paywalls, and full products. Every case study is backed by a real, running app.

  • Bizzscribe Shopping: The Same Store, in an App

    The shopping app that comes with a Bizzscribe store. One product page carries both a thing and a booking, and only the pair of buttons at the bottom changes.

    Mobile App
  • Bizzscribe Storefront: The Shop a Business Builds

    The ecommerce store a business builds on Bizzscribe and hands to its own customers, drawn for desktop and phone together. Nothing on the page belongs to the platform underneath.

    Web
  • LogiOps: A Control Center for Shipment Exceptions

    An internal logistics operations tool built with the Carbon design system. Find the delayed shipment, understand why, fix it from one place. Seven screens, light and dark from the same tokens.

    Web · Concept
  • Crate: A Transfer You Can Brand

    A service for sending files too big to email, where the thing being designed is the page the recipient opens, built beside you as you upload, then dressed in your own logo, colours and type.

    Web · Concept
  • HACA Design School: The Page That Sells the Course

    A branding course sold on a page that did not look branded. Display type, a colour per section, and the three things the page was missing: student work, the instructors, and the awkward questions about paying.

    Web · Concept
  • Chalo: A Trip You Can Describe

    A travel app where the planning happens in a conversation: describe the trip, get an itinerary back, and book the flight without leaving the chat. Two-sided: there is a partner app for the people supplying it.

    Mobile App · Concept
  • Ember: Habits and Streaks

    A habit tracker built around one number, the streak. Categories as chips, a setup form that fits in one scroll, and a month that shows a missed day without making a fuss of it.

    Mobile App · Concept
  • Trackwise: Where Is My Parcel?

    “Where is my order?” is a quarter of all e-commerce support tickets, and the answer already exists in a carrier API. Built inside Razorpay's real Blade design system: one timeline across every handover, and every persona, plan edge and failure state designed rather than assumed.

    Web · Prototype
  • KSRTC Booking: A Platform Redesign

    The bug in a booking funnel is its shape, not its styling. Rebuilding Kerala's state bus platform around one continuous surface that holds five steps, designed and shipped as a running SwiftUI app.

    Mobile App · Prototype
  • Parcel: Premium Courier Booking

    Booking a courier that feels as effortless as ordering a ride, designed and built end-to-end in native SwiftUI.

    Mobile App · Prototype
  • Route Planner: A Day of Deliveries

    A last-mile delivery app that holds a 120-stop day on one surface, designed and built as a running SwiftUI prototype.

    Mobile App · Prototype
  • ShipFlow: Shipment Operations Dashboard

    The job is not managing shipments, it is finding the ones going wrong. A multi-carrier operations dashboard that opens on triage, designed and coded end-to-end, with no framework and no build step.

    Web · Live

Nijin Muhammed, UI/UX and Product Designer in Kozhikode, Kerala, India.
